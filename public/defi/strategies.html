Delta Neutral with stETH + Pendle PT

Earn a fixed yield on your staked ETH without directional price exposure. The magic is that your stETH appreciates from staking rewards while your short PT position loses value slowly as it converges to par, netting you a predictable return.

Yield Arbitrage

Exploit yield spreads between lending protocols and liquidity pools. The core idea: borrow cheaply from one market (e.g., Compound at 3.5%), deploy into a higher-yielding pool (e.g., Curve USDC pool at 5.2%), and pocket the spread. This strategy only works when the spread is wide enough to cover gas costs and impermanent loss. On stablecoins, there's no impermanent loss — pure carry trading.

Pendle PT/YT Fixed Yield Hedge

Lock in a fixed yield by buying PT (Principal Tokens) on Pendle while earning variable yield from your staked ETH. The PT represents the principal portion of a yield-bearing asset — it trades at a discount to par based on time and implied yield, and converges to $1.00 at maturity. By holding PT instead of raw ETH, you eliminate yield variability and lock a known return. YT (Yield Tokens) give you variable yield exposure — if ETH staking rewards rise, YT holders capture that upside.

? Frequently Asked Questions

What's the safest DeFi strategy?

Using LSD (Liquid Staking Derivatives) like stETH as collateral is among the safest active strategies. You're earning staking yield (~4.2%) while maintaining exposure to ETH price appreciation. The risk is primarily smart contract risk and slight LTV liquidation risk at very high borrow ratios. Simple supply/borrow on established protocols (Aave, Compound) for stablecoins with a tight spread is also relatively low risk, especially if you avoid high leverage.

What's the highest-yield DeFi strategy?

Leveraged farming with 5–10x leverage on productive DeFi protocols historically delivered the highest yields, sometimes 20–50%+ APY. However, this comes with proportional risk: a 10–20% adverse price move at 10x leverage results in total liquidation. More sustainable high yields come from yield arbitrage (stablecoin carry trades), harness strategies on Pendle (combining staking yield + YT farming), and EigenLayer restaking which earns extra yields from protocol-level security services.

What is a health factor in DeFi lending?

The Health Factor (HF) is the ratio of your collateral value (adjusted by LTV) to your borrowed value. HF = (collateral × liquidation threshold) / borrowed value. On Aave, if your HF drops below 1.0, your position can be liquidated. Most protocols recommend maintaining HF above 1.5 for safety. A HF of 2.0 means your collateral is worth twice what you owe — significant buffer against price drops. Watch your HF constantly when using leverage.

What is delta neutral in DeFi?

Delta neutral means your net market exposure is approximately zero. In DeFi, this typically means holding a volatile asset (like stETH) while shorting a related instrument (like Pendle PT) in proportions that cancel out price movements. The result is a position that earns yield purely from the spread — you're insulated from ETH's price swings but exposed to changes in yield rates. Delta neutral strategies are popular among sophisticated traders who want to farm yield without directional bets.

What's impermanent loss (IL) and does it affect all strategies?

Impermanent loss occurs when you provide liquidity to an AMM (like Curve or Balancer) and the price of your assets diverges from when you deposited. It's called "impermanent" because it only becomes real loss when you withdraw. IL affects strategies involving LP positions — leveraged farming, yield arbitrage with non-stable pairs. It does NOT affect: simple lending/borrowing (no AMM exposure), LSD collateral strategies (no LP), or PT/YT positions on Pendle (AMM-free trading via PT/YT split). Stablecoin LP has essentially zero IL since all assets are pegged at $1.00.

How do I get started with these strategies?

Start small. Every protocol page on this site has an interactive sandbox — use those to build intuition before committing real capital. For beginners, the LSD Liquidity strategy (using stETH as collateral) is the most approachable: deposit stETH on Aave, borrow a small amount of stablecoin, and observe how your health factor changes. For yield arbitrage, use the simulators to find spread opportunities that exceed $100 annual net income — smaller than that and gas fees eat your profits. Never use more than 3x leverage until you deeply understand liquidation dynamics.
