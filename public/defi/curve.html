What is Curve Finance?

Curve Finance is a decentralized exchange purpose-built for stablecoins and pegged assets. Unlike a typical AMM like Uniswap — where any two tokens can be traded along the familiar x·y=k bonding curve — Curve uses a specialized formula called StableSwap that dramatically reduces slippage when the two assets are meant to be worth the same ($1 = $1, USDC ≈ USDT).

In a regular AMM, trading $1M of USDC for USDT would move you way down the hyperbola — you'd receive significantly less than $1M worth due to slippage. That's fine for arbitrary token pairs (who cares if your JPEG is worth a little less mid-trade?) but catastrophic for stablecoins where you expect penny-perfect execution. Stablecoins are supposed to stay at $1.00. Curve exploits that invariant.

The StableSwap formula combines the standard x·y=k hyperbola with a linear component (x + y = constant). The result: as long as the pool stays balanced near its peg, slippage is nearly flat — almost like a traditional order book. The curve only steepens sharply when the pool becomes extremely one-sided, which protects LPs from impermanent loss in normal conditions.

Curve also pioneered veCRV governance — a system where CRV token holders lock their tokens to gain voting power and boost their yield. The longer you lock, the more boost you receive (up to 2.5× for a 4-year lock). veCRV holders direct CRV emissions to pools they want to incentivize, effectively doing protocol-level governance of liquidity allocation. This makes Curve as much a financial infrastructure layer as a trading venue.

The protocol has expanded beyond simple stablecoin swaps into BasePools (multi-asset stablecoin pools like 3pool: USDC/USDT/DAI) and MetaPools (wrapping a non-stablecoin asset like ETH or stETH into a stablecoin pool for low-slippage trading against stables). This extensibility — keeping the same stablecoin math but layering new assets on top — is what makes Curve's TVL dominant in DeFi.

Glossary

StableSwap

Curve's specialized AMM formula combining x·y=k with a linear x+y=constant term, optimized for pegged assets that should trade near 1:1.

LP Token

A liquidity provider receipt token. When you deposit assets into a Curve pool, you receive LP tokens representing your share of the pool.

veCRV

Vote-escrowed CRV. Lock CRV to receive veCRV. Longer locks = more veCRV = higher yield boost and stronger voting power in protocol governance.

BasePool

A stablecoin pool containing 3 or more pegged assets (e.g., USDC, USDT, DAI in the 3pool). Minimizes slippage for multi-stablecoin swaps.

MetaPool

A pool that pairs a new asset (like ETH or a Frax variant) against an existing BasePool's LP token, enabling low-slippage trading without diluting the base pool.

Amplification Coefficient (A)

The "A parameter" controls how aggressively the StableSwap curve transitions from flat to steep. Higher A = tighter peg maintenance but more gas cost.
