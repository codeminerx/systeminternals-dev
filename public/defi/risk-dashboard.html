08 — Risk Glossary & FAQ

🛡️ Smart Contract Risk

The risk that a protocol's code contains bugs, vulnerabilities, or exploits that can lead to loss of funds. Mitigated by audits, bug bounties, and time in production.

👥 Counterparty Risk

The risk that another party in a financial transaction defaults or behaves dishonestly. In DeFi, this applies to centralized bridges, custodians, and institutional counterparties.

💧 Liquidity Risk

The risk of being unable to enter or exit a position at a fair price due to insufficient market depth. Higher in low-cap altcoin pools and newer protocols.

📋 Regulatory Risk

The risk that regulations restrict or ban certain DeFi activities, token types, or protocols. Higher for privacy coins, mixers, and protocols without clear legal status.

📉 Market Risk

Systemic risk from crypto market downturns, correlation across assets, and cascading liquidations. The primary cause of DeFi mega-liquidations in 2022.

⚖️ Health Factor

The ratio of collateral value to borrowed value. Below 1.0 triggers liquidation on Aave/Compound. Higher = safer position. Aim for HF > 1.5 as a safety margin.

💸 Impermanent Loss

Loss incurred by liquidity providers when the price of assets diverges from when they were deposited. "Impermanent" until the position is withdrawn. Stablecoin pools have near-zero IL.

🔗 Reentrancy Attack

An exploit where a malicious contract recursively calls a vulnerable function before the state updates. Famous example: The DAO hack (2016). Mitigated by checks-effects-interactions pattern.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the safest DeFi protocol?
No DeFi protocol is risk-free. However, Aave, MakerDAO, and Compound consistently rank highest in smart contract security due to extensive audits, bug bounties, and multi-year track records. Always DYOR and never invest more than you can afford to lose.
How is overall risk score calculated?
The overall score is a weighted average of 5 risk dimensions: Smart Contract Risk (30%), Counterparty Risk (20%), Liquidity Risk (20%), Regulatory Risk (10%), and Market Risk (20%). Each dimension is scored 1–10, where 1 is lowest risk.
What health factor should I maintain?
Most lending protocols liquidate positions below HF=1.0. We recommend maintaining HF > 1.5 as a safety margin to avoid liquidation during volatility. Some sophisticated users target HF > 2.0 for maximum safety.
Are audits enough to guarantee safety?
Audits are important but not sufficient. They represent a point-in-time review. Protocols evolve, new attack vectors emerge, and dependencies (oracles, bridges) can be compromised. Look for protocols with ongoing bug bounties, multiple audits from reputable firms, and strong security teams.
How do bridges compare on security?
ZK-based bridges (zkBridge, Succinct) generally offer stronger security guarantees than optimistic bridges, which rely on honest minority assumptions. Multisig bridges are simplest but concentrate trust in a small number of guardians. Always check the guardian set size and slashing conditions.
What was the biggest DeFi exploit?
The Ronin Bridge hack (March 2022) lost ~$625M. Other notable exploits: Wormhole ($320M), Poly Network ($611M), Euler Finance ($197M), and Mango Markets ($117M). Always use audited bridges and limit cross-chain exposure.
